## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development


## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace that directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers. The platform eliminates unnecessary middlemen by allowing farmers to list their agricultural products, set prices, and receive orders directly from buyers.

Key Features:
- Direct Marketplace: Farmers can list products, quantities, prices, and availability.
- AI-Based Demand Forecasting: Predicts future demand using previous sales, seasonal trends, and market data.
- Smart Logistics: Connects farmers with transportation providers for efficient product delivery.
- Bulk Ordering: Allows hotels, supermarkets, retailers, and businesses to purchase products in large quantities.
- Price Transparency: Displays market prices to help farmers make informed pricing decisions.
- Order Tracking: Enables farmers and buyers to monitor orders and delivery status.

## Technical Approach
The proposed platform uses modern web technologies, artificial intelligence, and database management to create an efficient agricultural marketplace.

Technologies:
Component	Technology
Frontend	React.js
Backend	Python, Django
Database	PostgreSQL
AI/ML	Python, Scikit-learn
Maps and Routes	Google Maps API
Authentication	JWT
Deployment	Cloud Hosting

Implementation Steps:
1. Farmers register and create their profiles.
2. Farmers upload product details, quantities, and prices.
3. Buyers browse available agricultural products.
4. Buyers place individual or bulk orders.
5. AI analyzes historical sales and market trends to forecast demand.
6. The logistics module identifies suitable delivery options.
7. Buyers track their orders until delivery.
8. The system collects transaction data to improve future predictions.

AI Integration:
- Demand prediction using historical purchasing patterns.
- Identification of high-demand agricultural products.
- Route optimization to reduce delivery time and transportation costs.
- Data-driven insights for farmers to make better selling decisions.



## Feasibility and Viability
The proposed solution is technically and economically feasible because it uses widely available technologies and existing digital infrastructure.

Feasibility:
- A web-based platform can be developed using open-source technologies.
- Farmers and buyers can access the platform through smartphones or computers.
- AI models can be trained using historical agricultural sales and demand data.
- Existing map services can support transportation and route planning.
Potential Challenges:
1. Limited digital awareness among some farmers.
2. Fluctuations in agricultural product prices.
3. Difficulty maintaining product freshness during transportation.
4. Inaccurate demand predictions due to changing market conditions.
5. Coordination between farmers, buyers, and logistics providers.
Solutions to Challenges:
- Provide a simple, multilingual, user-friendly interface.
- Integrate market price information.
- Partner with local transportation providers and cold-storage facilities.
- Continuously improve AI predictions using updated market data.
- Provide order tracking and transparent communication.
Viability:

The platform can generate revenue through small transaction commissions, logistics service fees, and subscription plans for bulk buyers. It can gradually expand to different regions and agricultural markets.
